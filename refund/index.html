Refund Policy

Effective 16 October 2026. Applies to Purple Party Pro and DualForge Pro, sold by Flags Software.

1. Who handles payments

Pro plans are sold through Lemon Squeezy, which is the Merchant of Record for every order. Lemon Squeezy takes the payment, charges any sales tax or VAT, issues the receipt and invoice, and pays out refunds. Its own terms also apply to the purchase.

2. 14-day refund on your first purchase

If you buy Pro for a product for the first time and it is not for you, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of the purchase date, for any reason. This covers a first monthly, yearly or lifetime purchase. The refund goes back to the original payment method; how long it takes to appear depends on your bank or card provider. After a refund, the license key is turned off and the app goes back to its free plan. Nothing you saved in the app is deleted.

Renewals and repeat purchases (for example buying again after a refund) are not covered by the 14-day refund, unless the law requires otherwise.

3. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel a monthly or yearly plan at any time from your Lemon Squeezy order page (app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders). Cancelling stops future charges, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. We do not refund the unused part of a period, except under section 2 or where the law requires it.

4. Game bans are not refundable (DualForge)

Some games and anti-cheat systems do not allow input-remapping software, virtual controllers, macros or turbo. A ban, suspension or other action a game publisher takes against your account or device is not grounds for a refund. Check each game's rules before you use DualForge there.

5. When a refund may be declined

We or Lemon Squeezy may decline a refund where there is clear abuse, such as repeated buy-and-refund cycles or sharing or reselling license keys.

6. Your legal rights

If you live in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you may have a legal right to withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days, and any refund the law requires is paid through Lemon Squeezy. Nothing in this policy takes away a right the law gives you.

7. How to ask for a refund

Use your Lemon Squeezy order page (app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders), or contact us through the contact section and say which product it is about. Please keep order numbers and keys out of public posts. Ask us before opening a dispute with your bank; a dispute may turn the key off while it is handled.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The version in force on the day you buy applies to that purchase.
