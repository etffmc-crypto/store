DualForge · Windows 10/11, 64-bit

Tune your DualSense. Every PC game sees an Xbox controller.

DualForge is a Windows app for the PlayStation 5 DualSense controller. It remaps buttons, shapes stick curves and deadzones, calibrates the sticks, and sets triggers, gyro, rumble and lights, with profiles you can switch. It drives a virtual Xbox controller, so PC games see your tuned input.

Go to the download page Free and Pro

What it does

  • Remapping. Map the DualSense's buttons and sticks to Xbox buttons and sticks.
  • Sticks. Response curves, deadzones and calibration.
  • Triggers, gyro, rumble and lights. Trigger modes, gyro to the right stick, rumble and lightbar settings.
  • Profiles. Keep different setups and switch between them.
  • Health page. Checks the drivers DualForge works with and installs them when you click Install.
  • No account. Your profiles, settings and logs stay on your PC.

Connect the DualSense with a USB cable; Bluetooth is not supported yet. The download page has the installer, a quick start and the checksums for each release.

Before you use DualForge in a game: some games and anti-cheat systems do not allow input-remapping software, virtual controllers, macros or turbo. Using DualForge in those games is at your own risk and can lead to a block or ban. Check each game's rules before you use it there. DualForge is not endorsed by any game publisher or platform, and a game ban is not grounds for a refund.
